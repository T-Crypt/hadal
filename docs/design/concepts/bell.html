Steel and readout

plate 0 plate 1 plate 2 signal ok warn err
  • Surfaces are desaturated steel plates, three steps; blue only survives in the signal. Radius 3px, hairlines do the structure, no shadows except the frame.
  • Type: Geist 14–15 for reading, Plex Mono for every label, key, figure and log line. Sentence case, lowercase in the rail — the rail reads like output.

What changes from today

  • Environments lead the navigator. Folders and machines are the primary tree (the operator's framing: it organises environments, agents, capabilities and activity); sessions sit inside, flagged when live.
  • The top rail is one readout: env, model, thinking, a ten-step context meter, cache, spend, what Lantern is doing, who is here, agents, schedules — always true, never a tooltip hunt.
  • The composer is a console tray: labeled segmented knobs for model, thinking, plan and browser instead of floating chips; the same controls, all states visible.
  • Panels dock as portholes (changes, files, people here) — nothing modal on desktop; sheets stay on phones.
  • Tool calls render as an aligned log, one grid: kind, argument, result in tabular figures.

Honest risks

  • Density: 14–15px type and logs read well at a desk, and the phone mock keeps 44px targets and 15px prose — but this direction wants a watch on small-phone reading comfort.
  • The steel plates depart most from the blue depth palette; every Omarchy palette still applies, this only sets the default geometry and structure.
  • The always-on rail means eight live figures need a quiet error story (what shows when spend is unknown).
